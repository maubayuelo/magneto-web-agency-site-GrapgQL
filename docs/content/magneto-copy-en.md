#### Start with the problem — not a package.

Magneto helps Montreal service businesses choose the most sensible path for their website.

The three core services are **BUILD, FIX and CARE**.

# Core Services

### 3. BUILD — Website Build / Rebuild

#### Build a website that makes your business easier to understand, trust and contact.

BUILD is for businesses that need a new website, a substantial rebuild, or a focused landing page.

The work brings strategy, structure, UX/UI and development together so the finished website works as one coherent experience.

**Typical scope can include:**

- Website strategy and content structure
- UX/UI design
- Responsive development
- CMS when needed
- Forms, booking and standard integrations
- Multilingual implementation when required
- Analytics and search foundations
- QA, launch and handoff

**Investment:** Starting at **C\$3,500** for a focused one-language landing page. Typical website investment: **C\$5,000–8,000**. Custom projects: **C\$10,000+**. All prices are in Canadian dollars. Applicable taxes are added when required.

**CTA:** See BUILD pricing

### 4. FIX — Website Optimization & Rescue

#### Improve the website you already have — without rebuilding it unnecessarily.

FIX is for websites that are fundamentally usable but have specific problems affecting clarity, usability or reliability.

That may include a confusing mobile experience, slow pages, broken forms or booking, WordPress or other technical problems, analytics issues, accessibility foundations or other technical friction.

Magneto follows a simple process:

**Diagnose → Prioritize → Approve → Fix → Verify**

You know what is being fixed before the work starts, and the result is checked afterward.

**Common FIX work can include:**

- Mobile and usability problems
- Forms and booking issues
- Performance improvements
- WordPress or other technical problems
- Analytics setup or corrections
- Accessibility foundations
- Technical reliability issues

Most bounded issues can move directly to a FIX estimate. If the website needs a deeper technical review before the work can be responsibly scoped, a Paid Website Diagnostic may be recommended first.

**CTA:** See how FIX works

### 5. CARE — Website Care

#### Keep your website maintained, monitored and supported.

CARE is ongoing support for websites that are already in good working condition.

Depending on the plan, CARE can include routine maintenance, monitoring, critical form or booking-flow checks, website health review, small changes and ongoing website support.

Sites built by Magneto can move into CARE after launch when appropriate. If an existing site was not built by Magneto, a Paid Website Diagnostic is required before CARE acceptance and any existing problems may need to be stabilized through FIX first.

**CTA:** Compare CARE plans

# Included Where Your Project Needs It

These are supporting capabilities, not separate Magneto packages. They are included or scoped when they support the website and business goal.

### 6. Focused Landing Pages

#### One audience. One offer. One clear next step.

A focused landing page is a **BUILD project** for a specific service, campaign or offer when an entire website is not required.

It can include focused page structure, UX/UI designed around one clear next step, forms or booking, analytics foundations and multilingual implementation when needed.

Landing pages can support referrals, search or paid campaigns. Magneto builds the page; ad campaign management is not included.

### 7. Multilingual Websites

#### Serve your audience clearly in English, French and Spanish.

Multilingual implementation can include adapting approved website copy between English, French and Spanish when included in the project scope.

Magneto can structure and build multilingual experiences while keeping navigation, content hierarchy and calls to action consistent across languages.

Language scope affects project complexity and is assessed as part of the applicable BUILD, FIX or CARE work.

Certified or specialized translation is not included. Final translated copy is subject to client approval.

### 8. Booking, Forms & Lead Capture

#### Make it easier for the right visitor to take the next step.

Depending on the business, the website can connect visitors to contact forms, booking systems, Calendly, newsletter or email capture, WhatsApp and other standard inquiry flows.

The goal is not to add tools for their own sake. The right flow depends on what the visitor needs to do next.

### 9. Analytics, Search & Accessibility Foundations

#### Give the website the technical basics it needs to work responsibly.

When relevant to the project, Magneto can include analytics setup, search / SEO foundations, accessibility foundations, CMS configuration and standard third-party integrations.

Advanced automation, AI systems, complex e-commerce, custom application logic or unusual infrastructure require separate assessment and may become a Custom project.
